What do these columns mean?
Hour — every row covers the 60 minutes ENDING at the time shown, so Up to 9 AM is 8:00 through 8:59.
TY vs LY · Running Total — cumulative demand from midnight through each hour, against the comparable day last year at the same point.
This Hour Only — demand from that single hour, not cumulative, with the dollar difference against the same hour last year.
Hrly YOY% — year over year for that single hour. A run of green rows is the earliest signal of a turn.
Momentum — change in the cumulative YOY% versus the prior hour.
Shape — the last eight hours of hourly demand, this year as bars and last year as the faint line behind them.
% of LY Day — how far along today is toward beating the comparable day's full total. Crossing 100% means today beat that entire day.
